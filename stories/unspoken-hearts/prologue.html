PROLOGUE

Unspoken Hearts

Unspoken Hearts · Victor Blackthorn

Now readingPrologue
Text

Love is a strange thing. Sometimes, it arrives like a storm—loud, chaotic, impossible to ignore.

Other times, it lingers in the quiet spaces, unnoticed, waiting for the right moment to be seen.

For Aarav Sen, love had always been silent. A quiet ache buried beneath laughter, a stolen glance across a crowded room, a wish that remained unspoken. He had loved Kavya Sharma for as long as he could remember—not the kind of love that demanded grand gestures or dramatic confessions, but the kind that settled deep in his heart, growing stronger with time.

Kavya, with her bright smile and carefree spirit, moved through life as if she belonged to the wind—untamed, always reaching for something beyond her grasp. And Aarav? He was the boy who stood at the edge of her world, watching, waiting, hoping.

But love is never simple.

Because while Aarav longed for Kavya, someone else longed for him.

And in the quiet corners of their story, where words were left unspoken and feelings went unnoticed, hearts would break, choices would be made, and love—the kind that changes everything—would demand to be heard.

This is a story of love, longing, and the whispers of a heart too afraid to speak.
