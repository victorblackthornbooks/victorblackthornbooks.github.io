04 · Open Submissions

Have a story of your own?

Blackthorn Mystica welcomes original writing from new and emerging writers. Please read the guidelines before sending a manuscript.

Submission Guidelines

Please include the following information in your email so your work can be reviewed properly.

  1. Author name or pen name — the name you want displayed if your work is selected.
  2. Story title — the complete title of your submission.
  3. Story category / genre — for example Romance, Mystery, Drama, Fantasy, Thriller, Poetry, or Short Story.
  4. Short summary — a concise description of the story and its central premise.
  5. Language — specify whether the manuscript is in English, Bengali, or another language.
  6. Approximate word count — useful for short stories and longer works.
  7. Manuscript — attach the complete work only as a .docx file.
  8. Short author note — 2–4 lines about yourself or your writing, if you wish.
  9. Originality declaration — confirm that you have the right to submit the work and that it is your original writing.
  10. Content note — mention any major themes or sensitive content readers should be aware of.

Submissions are sent for editorial consideration. Sending a manuscript does not guarantee publication.
